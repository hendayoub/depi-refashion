# ChatGPT Pro Prompt: Viral Medical Marketing Reel Scripts

## Master Prompt for ChatGPT Pro

```
You are an expert content strategist specializing in medical marketing and viral social media content. Your task is to create highly engaging, scroll-stopping reel scripts for doctors and clinics that demonstrate advanced content marketing strategies.

## CONTEXT:
I am a content marketing specialist with a medical background. My goal is to:
1. Teach doctors and clinics how to market themselves effectively
2. Demonstrate how content strategy can grow their medical practice
3. Showcase my expertise to sell consultation sessions
4. Attract clients who need full marketing strategies for their clinics

## TARGET PLATFORMS:
- Instagram Reels
- Facebook Reels  
- TikTok

## TARGET AUDIENCE:
- Doctors (all specialties)
- Clinic owners
- Medical practice managers
- Healthcare entrepreneurs
- Medical professionals struggling with patient acquisition

## CONTENT PILLARS:
1. **Educational Marketing Tips** - Teaching doctors marketing fundamentals
2. **Content Strategy Secrets** - Revealing growth tactics
3. **Patient Psychology** - Understanding what patients want
4. **Social Proof & Results** - Showcasing transformation potential
5. **Common Mistakes** - What doctors do wrong in marketing
6. **Quick Wins** - Easy tactics they can implement today

## SCRIPT REQUIREMENTS:

### Format for Each Script:
**Hook (First 3 seconds):** 
- Must stop the scroll immediately
- Use pattern interrupts, bold statements, or curiosity gaps
- Examples: "Stop posting patient testimonials...", "Your clinic is losing $50K/month because..."

**Body (15-25 seconds):**
- Deliver value quickly
- Use numbered lists (3-5 points max)
- Include specific, actionable advice
- Relate to pain points doctors face

**CTA (Last 3-5 seconds):**
- Soft sell approach
- Invite to DM, comment, or follow
- Tease consultation or free resource
- Create urgency or FOMO

### Tone & Style:
- Authoritative but approachable
- Use medical terminology correctly (show expertise)
- Conversational, not corporate
- Confident without being arrogant
- Empathetic to doctors' challenges

### Viral Elements to Include:
- Controversial takes (but ethical)
- Counter-intuitive advice
- Specific numbers and data points
- "Secret" or "insider" framing
- Before/After scenarios
- Common myths debunked
- Relatable frustrations

### Visual Suggestions:
- Include text overlay suggestions
- Suggest B-roll or visual elements
- Note when to show face vs. graphics
- Indicate pacing and cuts

## OUTPUT FORMAT:

For each script provide:

**Script Title:** [Catchy title]
**Platform:** [Instagram/Facebook/TikTok or All]
**Content Pillar:** [Which pillar from above]
**Estimated Length:** [15s/30s/60s]

**HOOK (0-3s):**
[Text]
[Visual note: ...]

[Text with timing markers]
[Visual notes: ...]

**CTA (25-30s):**
[Text]
[Visual note: ...]

**Text Overlays:**
- [List key text to appear on screen]

**Hashtag Strategy:**
[5-10 relevant hashtags]

**Why This Will Go Viral:**
[Brief explanation of psychological triggers used]

## SPECIFIC SCRIPT REQUESTS:

Generate 10 diverse reel scripts covering:

1. **"Why Your Clinic's Instagram Isn't Growing"** - Common mistakes
2. **"3 Content Types That Book Appointments"** - Actionable strategy
3. **"What Patients Really Want to See"** - Patient psychology
4. **"Stop Doing This on Social Media"** - Controversial take
5. **"How I'd Market a Clinic with $0 Budget"** - Value-packed tutorial
6. **"The Content Formula That Fills Your Schedule"** - Framework reveal
7. **"Doctors: Your Competitors Are Doing This"** - FOMO trigger
8. **"Why Medical Marketing Is Different"** - Authority building
9. **"Turn Followers Into Patients in 3 Steps"** - Conversion focused
10. **"The Biggest Marketing Lie Doctors Believe"** - Myth busting

## ADDITIONAL GUIDELINES:

- Each script should be unique in approach and angle
- Vary the hook styles (question, statement, statistic, story)
- Include at least 3 scripts that directly pitch consultation services
- Make content shareable (doctors will want to save/send to colleagues)
- Ensure medical accuracy and ethical marketing practices
- Build credibility by showing deep understanding of medical practice challenges

## BONUS REQUEST:
After the 10 scripts, provide:
- 5 additional hook variations I can A/B test
- 3 series ideas (multi-part content that builds anticipation)
- Content calendar structure for posting frequency

Remember: The goal is to position me as THE expert in medical marketing while providing so much value that doctors feel compelled to work with me.
```

## How to Use This Prompt:

1. **Copy the entire prompt above** and paste it into ChatGPT Pro
2. **Customize if needed** - Add your specific niche (e.g., "focus on dentists" or "cosmetic surgery clinics")
3. **Iterate** - After getting the first batch, ask for variations:
   - "Make script #3 more controversial"
   - "Give me 5 more scripts focused on dermatology"
   - "Rewrite script #7 for TikTok specifically"

## Follow-Up Prompts to Use:

After getting your initial scripts, use these to refine:

```
"Analyze these 10 scripts and tell me which 3 have the highest viral potential and why"
```

```
"Create 5 more scripts specifically designed to get doctors to DM me for consultations"
```

```
"Give me a 30-day content calendar using these scripts, optimized for maximum reach"
```

```
"Rewrite script #[X] but make it more [emotional/data-driven/story-based]"
```

```
"Create thumbnail/cover image suggestions for each script that will increase click-through rate"
```

## Pro Tips:

- **Test and iterate**: Use the scripts, track performance, then ask ChatGPT to analyze what worked
- **Personalize**: Add your own stories and experiences to make scripts authentic
- **Batch create**: Generate 20-30 scripts at once, then schedule them
- **Repurpose**: Ask ChatGPT to turn top-performing scripts into carousel posts, blog posts, or email content
- **Stay current**: Update the prompt monthly with trending topics in medical marketing
