- Business outcomes emphasized
- Minimal to no emojis

### Structure for Each Post:

**HOOK (First Line):**
- Must capture attention in 1-2 sentences
- Use curiosity gaps, bold statements, or questions
- Platform-specific approach:
  - Facebook: Emotional or story-based hook
  - Instagram: Visual or aspirational hook
  - LinkedIn: Data or insight-based hook

**BODY (Main Content):**
- Deliver substantial value
- Use storytelling merged with keywords naturally
- Include specific, actionable insights
- Address pain points doctors face
- Incorporate relevant keywords seamlessly:
  - Medical marketing
  - Content strategy
  - Patient acquisition
  - Healthcare branding
  - Practice growth
  - Digital marketing for doctors
  - Medical practice management
  - Patient engagement
  - Healthcare content
  - Clinic marketing strategy

**STORYTELLING ELEMENTS:**
- Use narrative arc (problem → insight → solution)
- Include real scenarios doctors face
- Create emotional connection
- Use metaphors and analogies
- Show transformation potential

**CTA (Call-to-Action):**
- Platform-specific approach:
  - Facebook: Soft invitation to comment or share experiences
  - Instagram: Direct action (DM, save, share, click link in bio)
  - LinkedIn: Professional invitation (connect, discuss, schedule call)
- Create urgency without being pushy
- Offer value in exchange for action

### Viral Elements to Integrate:

- **Controversy** (ethical and professional)
- **Counter-intuitive insights** that challenge common beliefs
- **Specific numbers and statistics**
- **"Secret" or "insider" framing**
- **Before/After scenarios**
- **Myth-busting content**
- **Relatable frustrations**
- **Aspirational outcomes**
- **Social proof indicators**
- **Scarcity and urgency**

### Keyword Integration Strategy:

- Weave keywords naturally into storytelling
- Use semantic variations (not keyword stuffing)
- Include keywords in:
  - Opening hook
  - Body paragraphs
  - Hashtags (Instagram)
  - Closing CTA
- Maintain readability and flow
- Keywords should enhance, not disrupt narrative
